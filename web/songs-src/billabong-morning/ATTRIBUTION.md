`billabong-morning.mid` is written by `pnpm generate-song billabong-morning` from the
parameters in `generate.json` (seed, key, tempo, form, phrase length); running it again
gives the same bytes, and a test checks that. The generator (`scripts/songgen/`) picks
common chord progressions and rule-based melody steps with a seeded PRNG.
Track `melody` becomes the tiles; track `accompaniment` (the left hand) is the
accompaniment.
