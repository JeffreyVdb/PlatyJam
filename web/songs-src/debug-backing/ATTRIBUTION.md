A test song for the engine's backing-recording playback; it is in the debug catalog
(`?debug`) and never in song select for players.

`debug-backing.mid` is written by `pnpm generate-song debug-backing` from `generate.json`.
`public/songs/debug-backing.m4a` is the recording, written by `pnpm backing-fixture`
(`scripts/generate-backing-fixture.ts`): a click on every beat (higher and louder on the
downbeat, so drift is audible) over a steady D4 tone (the song's key, so the notes the
player hits can be heard in or out of tune with it at every speed). It is synthesised, not
sampled.
